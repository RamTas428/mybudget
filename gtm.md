## 7. Launch funnel

1. **Discover:** Audience encounters a helpful message or recommendation.
2. **Evaluate:** Landing page makes the audience, value, limitations, and next step clear.
3. **Start:** User opens or signs up for the app with minimal friction.
4. **Activate:** User enters income and expenses and sees a useful balance or projection.
5. **Return:** User has a reason to revisit and update their budget.
6. **Recommend:** Satisfied users can share feedback or invite others.
